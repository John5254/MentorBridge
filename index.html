MentorBridge NG is a gig-economy platform that connects verified NYSC members with community learning opportunities while building their professional portfolios.

Login Get Started

The Challenge

Two critical national problems we solve at once

1. Out-of-School Children Crisis

Millions of Nigerian children lack access to basic literacy, numeracy and soft skills due to economic and geographic barriers.

2. NYSC Value Realization Gap

Over 300,000 corps members are deployed yearly, many struggling to find meaningful CDS projects that provide real experience and career value.

How It Works

Simple, structured and impactful

1

Complete Mandatory Training

Corps members must finish the “Child Safeguarding & Ethics 101” course before accessing any gigs.

2

Discover & Claim Gigs

Browse community learning opportunities posted by Field Officers and claim the ones that match your location and interest.

3

Deliver & Submit Proof

Conduct the sessions, upload attendance sheets and photos/videos as proof of work.

4

Get Rated & Build Portfolio

Earn ratings, digital badges, service hours and a public portfolio you can use after NYSC.

Key Features

📚

NGO Skills Academy

Short, practical courses on child protection, facilitation and community development.

🛒

Gig Marketplace

Field officers post learning gigs. Corps members discover and claim them easily.

✅

Proof & Verification

Simple submission of attendance links + media. Admins review and approve.

👤

Public Portfolio
